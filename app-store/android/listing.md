## Full description (max 4000 chars)

833 Tidyups Booking is the fastest way for our team to lock in a new cleaning appointment — while the customer is still on the call.

BOOK IN UNDER 60 SECONDS
Tap through service type, home size, extras, and schedule with big, thumb-friendly controls. A live price estimate updates as you go, so you can quote on the spot.

EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
The dashboard shows revenue, pending and completed jobs, and every booking coming up in the next two weeks.

STAY ON TOP OF EVERY JOB
Browse all bookings, filter by status, and open any job to see the full details — address, home size, extras, and notes. Update the status with one tap or call the customer straight from the booking.

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
• Standard, deep, move in/out, and post-construction cleans
• Extras like inside fridge, oven, windows, and laundry
• One-time, weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly frequencies
• Instant price estimates
• Tap-to-call and tap-to-email customers

833 Tidyups Booking is the internal booking tool for 833 Tidyups, Edmonton's home cleaning service.
