# Privacy Policy — 833 Tidyups Booking

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

833 Tidyups Booking ("the App") is an internal booking tool operated by 833 Tidyups, a home cleaning service based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

## Information We Collect

The App is used by 833 Tidyups staff to record customer booking information provided over the phone, including:

- Customer name, phone number, and email address
- Service address (street, city, postal code)
- Booking details: service type, home size, extras, schedule, price estimate, and notes

The App does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data from the device.

## How Information Is Used

Booking information is used solely to schedule, manage, and deliver cleaning services. It is stored securely and is accessible only to authorized 833 Tidyups staff.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent customer information. Booking data may be shared with service providers we use to operate the business (such as scheduling and invoicing software) strictly to deliver our services.

## Data Retention & Deletion

Booking records are retained for as long as needed for business and legal purposes. Customers may request access to or deletion of their personal information by contacting us.
